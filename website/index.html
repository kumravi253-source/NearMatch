Trust & Safety

Safety isn't a checkbox here — it's the architecture

Most of the engineering effort in NearMatch went into the parts you never see directly.

  • 18+

    Explicit age attestation

    Every signup requires an affirmative, timestamped 18+ confirmation — recorded, not assumed.

  • 🤳

    Optional AI selfie verification

    A face-detection check estimates age conservatively and only confirms a badge when it clears a safety margin above 18. The photo is never stored.

  • ⛔

    Real blocking, enforced everywhere

    Blocking isn't just a UI filter — it's checked at the database level for matching and messaging, so a blocked pair can't reconnect through any path.

  • 🚩

    One-tap reporting

    Report inappropriate photos, harassment, fake profiles, or spam directly from any profile or conversation.

  • ⏱️

    Rate limiting against spam & bots

    Swipes and messages are throttled server-side, making large-scale spam or scraping impractical.

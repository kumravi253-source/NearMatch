Our mission

Warm connections, built on trust

Most dating apps optimise for engagement metrics — swipes, sessions, time-in-app. We optimise for something harder to measure: whether two real people actually connect.

That means verified profiles, not just flagged ones. Rate limiting that stops bots, not just slows them down. Blocking enforced at the database level, not just hidden in a UI filter.

NearMatch is the app we wished existed when we started looking.

What we stand for

Our values

🛡️

Safety first, always

Every feature is designed with safety as a constraint, not an afterthought. If it can be abused, we've already thought about it.

🤝

Honest about what we are

No fake countdown timers, no dark patterns, no manufactured urgency. We tell users what the app does and how it works.

🔬

Real infrastructure

Live backend, real matching logic, actual rate limiting. Nothing in NearMatch is a mockup that stops working under load.

💛

People over metrics

We measure success by quality of connections, not time spent swiping. A great match is better than a hundred mediocre ones.
